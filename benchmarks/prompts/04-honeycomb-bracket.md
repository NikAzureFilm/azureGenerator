# Honeycomb Right Angle Bracket

Create a 90-degree L bracket made from two rectangular legs, each 80 mm long, 30 mm wide, and 6 mm thick, joined with a 6 mm inside radius fillet at the corner. Lighten both legs with a repeating honeycomb pattern of through-cuts using 8 mm flat-to-flat hex cells and 2 mm ribs, while leaving a 7 mm solid border around all edges. Add four M5 clearance mounting holes, two on each leg, with hole centers 18 mm and 58 mm from the corner along each leg and centered across the 30 mm width.
