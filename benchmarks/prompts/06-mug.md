# Simple Mug

Model a simple cylindrical mug that is 82 mm outside diameter and 96 mm tall with a 3 mm wall thickness and an open top. Add a 5 mm thick base, a gently rounded 2 mm lip, and a C-shaped handle on one side with 12 mm round grip thickness, 42 mm internal height, and 24 mm internal clearance from the mug wall. Make the handle blend smoothly into the body near 25 mm and 72 mm above the bottom.
