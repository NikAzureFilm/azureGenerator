# Knurled Control Knob

Model a knurled control knob for a 6 mm D-shaped shaft. The knob should be 32 mm in diameter and 20 mm tall, with a 6.0 mm round bore that has a 1.5 mm flat cut along one side to form the D profile. Add 36 evenly spaced diamond or straight knurls around the outer grip, a shallow 1 mm bevel on the top and bottom edges, and an M3 set-screw hole drilled radially through the side with its center 9 mm above the bottom.
