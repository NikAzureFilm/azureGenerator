# Planetary Gear Stage

Build a simple printable planetary gear stage with a 20 tooth sun gear, three 30 tooth planet gears, and an 80 tooth internal ring gear. Use module 1.5 teeth, 12 mm face width, 20 degree pressure angle, and real involute gear tooth shapes if available. Arrange the planets evenly at 120 degree intervals around the sun, mesh them correctly with the fixed internal ring, add 3 mm axle holes through the planets, an 8 mm center bore through the sun, and show all gears in one assembled stage without intersecting solids.
