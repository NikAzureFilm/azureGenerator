# M12 Hex Bolt And Nut

Generate an M12 hex bolt and matching hex nut with real printable threads using BOSL2 threading helpers where appropriate. The bolt needs an M12 x 1.75 external thread, 45 mm threaded shaft length, 19 mm across-flats hex head, 7.5 mm head height, and a 1 mm chamfer on the head and thread start. Place a separate matching nut beside it with an M12 x 1.75 internal thread, 19 mm across-flats hex body, 10 mm thickness, and chamfers on both faces so the two parts are clearly visible but not intersecting.
