# Twisted Hexagonal Vase

Create a printable twisted hexagonal vase that is 160 mm tall with a 2.2 mm wall thickness, a flat 62 mm across-flats base, a 46 mm across-flats top opening, and a continuous 120 degree twist from bottom to top. Keep the inside hollow, leave the top open, add a 4 mm thick solid base disk, and slightly round the outside vertical edges while preserving the six-sided shape.
